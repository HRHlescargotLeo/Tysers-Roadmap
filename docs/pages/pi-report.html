Tysers P&I Report 2026: Who is Pitch Perfect?

Our annual analysis of the International Group of P&I Clubs and the trends shaping the global P&I market: club performance, financial strength, claims activity, underwriting trends and the issues facing shipowners and P&I insurers.

Download the full report (PDF, [size])Compare the clubs
Video: Tom Wilson, CEO, introduces the report (captions on)

What we're proposing

A permanent home for the report at one address, /pi-report/, that carries this year's edition and keeps the previous ones underneath. The headline findings are readable on the page, the clubs can be sorted and compared, and the PDF is one of several ways in rather than the only one.

Next year's edition replaces this one at the same address, so links from the market keep working.

Why

The report is described on the P&I page as one of the most comprehensive assessments of the P&I landscape. Online, the 2026 edition is a news post of about 100 words with a "here" link to the PDF, and each year's edition sits at a different address.

It is probably the most-read thing Tysers publishes, and currently it can't be found by anything except its PDF.
