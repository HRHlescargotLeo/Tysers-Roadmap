Specialty

Protection & Indemnity

Widely recognised as leaders in marine liability insurance, our Protection & Indemnity (P&I) team maintains trusted partnerships with International Group P&I Clubs and key fixed-premium P&I facilities around the world.

Talk to the teamMeet the team

What we're proposing

A specialty page built around evidence and people. Every one of the 79 specialties uses this template: the specific facts first, what the cover includes, the whole team with contact details, the claims lead for that class, related insight and an enquiry form that already knows which specialty it came from.

Open any other specialty from the A to Z or the menu and the same template fills from its own content. Sections the live page doesn't have yet show as placeholders.

Why

On the live P&I page, "Meet the team" shows one name and a title with no way to contact him. The same paragraph ("At Tysers, we combine deep sector knowledge with innovative thinking…") appears on this page, Cyber, Claims and the homepage.

The most persuasive lines on the live specialty pages are the specific ones, such as the 70 carriers and $500m+ of capacity on Cyber, so this template asks editors for those first.

Expert support for complex liabilities

Drawing on broad access to global insurance markets, we deliver tailored liability solutions aligned with the evolving risk profiles of shipowners and marine operators.

Our international client portfolio spans every segment of the maritime industry, from large blue-water shipping companies and offshore energy to specialised local and regional operators. Whether supporting a single vessel or a multinational fleet, we combine technical expertise, global reach and hands-on service.
