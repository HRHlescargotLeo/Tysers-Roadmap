What we're proposing

The live site's Tysers UK, Business Insurance and High-Net-Worth pages brought together as one division, on the same template as the others, with a notice at the top that can carry whatever Tysers decides to tell clients about Prestige.

If these pages move to Prestige, this becomes a short hand-off page with redirects underneath, and nothing else in the site needs to change. Specialties are tagged by division, so they drop out of every list at once.

Why

We don't know the timetable for the retail integration, or whether these pages are meant to stay on tysers.com. The model works either way, which seemed the sensible thing to build until somebody tells us.

The live site also calls this division three different names ("Tysers UK" in the menu, "Business Insurance" and "High-Net-Worth Insurance" on the homepage).

For UK businesses

Our specialists work with organisations of every size and sector, from small local businesses to large national corporations. Larger clients get a dedicated account manager; smaller businesses get specialist advisers by phone or online.
