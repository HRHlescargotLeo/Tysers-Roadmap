About Tysers

Tysers began in the City of London in 1820, in the marine trade of the developing world. Originally a family firm, then a private partnership, it incorporated in 2001. As a Lloyd's broker we have access to Lloyd's of London and the specialist company markets trading out of London.

History highlights

  1. 1820The company now known as Tysers is founded in the City of London.
  2. 1870George Dorman Tyser is appointed by Parliament to the first committee of Lloyd's of London.
  3. 1914Tyser & Co handles all insurance requirements of the New Zealand Expeditionary Forces in the First World War.
  4. 1979Tyser UK Ltd is formed to handle UK business interests.
  5. 2018Tysers is bought by Integro Insurance Brokers.
  6. 2019UK corporate and private client business is consolidated under the Tysers brand, and RFIB is acquired.
  7. 2020Integro's Entertainment & Sport team comes under the Tysers brand, completing the move to one Tysers. Tysers turns 200.
  8. 2022Tysers becomes a member of the AUB Group.
