Insights

Research, market updates, claims stories and guides from our specialists.

What we're proposing

Insight separated from people news, filterable by type, division and year, with the flagship report pinned at the top. Every item has a type, a reading time and a named author from the People library.

Two items here are placeholders for content that doesn't exist yet: a quarterly market update per practice, which is the cheapest way to keep a specialty site looking alive.

Why

News & Insights on the live site mixes appointments, reports and private-client motor articles. Its categories are News, Marine & Aviation, Prestige Motor and Corporate & Commercial, so nothing can be filed under Tysers Live, Management Risk or Reinsurance.

The first page jumps from 29 January to 13 August 2026. One article in the news sitemap, "Making waves in marine claims", now returns a 404.
