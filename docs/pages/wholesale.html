Wholesale & Specialty

Extraordinary risks demand exceptional protection. As a Lloyd's broker, our specialists work with established markets to build well-structured placements for even the most complex risks, and support clients and brokers from negotiating cover through to claims.

What we're proposing

One landing page per division, each built from the same blocks: what the division does, its practices and every specialty under them, the people who lead it, its claims team and its latest insight.

The practice list is generated from the Specialties tree in Umbraco, so a new product page appears here without anyone editing this page.

Why

The live Wholesale menu holds about 30 links, and the Wholesale page itself lists the six practices with no route to the products under them or to anyone in the team. A broker who wants to know who handles hull war cover has to guess.

Reinsurance that helps manage volatility and support long-term growth

We design and place flexible reinsurance programmes, including treaty, facultative and hybrid integrated structures, to improve capital efficiency and strengthen operational resilience. We support all forms of proportional and non-proportional reinsurance, including structured solutions, index and parametric products, and hybrid treaty-fac programmes.
