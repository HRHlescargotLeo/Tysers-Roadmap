Entertainment, media and sport

You provide the show. We provide the cover.

Tysers Live brings together our entertainment and sport expertise under one brand, designing insurance programmes for individuals, companies and organisations working in artistic creation and athletic competition.

What we're proposing

The same division template as Wholesale & Specialty, filled with Tysers Live's practices, people and claims teams, plus a short quote request for events and productions.

Why

On the live Festival page, "Get a Quote" jumps to the team block further down the same page. Tysers Live clients are often booking cover against a date, and a form that captures the date, the venue and the audience size gets the team most of what it needs.
