What we're proposing

A claims page that does three jobs in order: emergency numbers first, then an online notification that returns a reference number, then a finder that shows the right team for your division and class.

The full contact list is still here, below, verified and with hours, for anyone who would rather pick up the phone.

Why

The live Claims page is a list of 14 phone routes with no online notification. The "Claims – London Office" number reads 0120 675 6033 and dials 01206 756033, which is the Colchester claims line. Two directors share one mobile number.

Someone reporting a loss is having a bad day already, and this is the page where the site most needs to be right.
