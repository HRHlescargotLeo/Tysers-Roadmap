What we're proposing

A homepage whose first job is routing. The hero is cut down to one line and two actions, and straight underneath it a visitor chooses who they are, searches for a specialty or a person, or goes directly to notifying a claim.

The video stays, if Tysers wants it, but as a short compressed cut with a poster frame and a pause button.

Why

On the live homepage the four service cards sit about three screens down, after a full-height 15.6MB video and the about text. The headline figures are in the HTML as "$0Bn" and "0+" until a script counts them up, so search engines and screen readers read zeros.

Here the figures are printed as they are and the routes come first.
