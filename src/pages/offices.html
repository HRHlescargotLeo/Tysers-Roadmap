Offices

Headquartered at 70 Fenchurch Street, close to Lloyd's, with offices and representatives across the UK, Europe, the Middle East, Asia and Australasia.

What we're proposing

Every Tysers location on one page, filterable by region, each with its address, phone, email, what it does, its regulator and a link to the people based there. Offices are content nodes, so the same record feeds this page, the contact page and each person's profile.

Why

Global Offices lists the UK, Bangladesh, Belgium, Dubai, Saudi Arabia and Singapore. Pages for Hong Kong, Japan and Ireland exist and are indexed but aren't in that list, and Regulatory Status names Australia and New Zealand, which appear nowhere else.
