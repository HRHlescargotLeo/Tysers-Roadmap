What we're proposing

One profile per person, generated from their People record. Try another name from the directory and the same template fills with their details.

Everything after the biography is automatic: specialties come from the specialty pages they're picked on, and insight from the articles that name them as author.

Why

Today these URLs exist and are empty. Filled in, they become the pages a client finds when they search for a broker's name, which is how a fair amount of specialty business probably starts.
