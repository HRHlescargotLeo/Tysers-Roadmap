Shipping and maritime

Our international client portfolio spans every segment of the maritime industry, from large blue-water shipping companies and offshore energy to specialised local and regional operators. Whether we're supporting a single vessel or a multinational fleet, the cover, the claims team and the research all come from one place.

What we're proposing

Industry hubs that sit across the product structure. This one gathers every specialty a shipowner or operator might need, the people who place and claim it, and the P&I research, on one page.

Nothing here is written twice. The specialties, people and insight are pulled in by tag, so the hub stays current as those pages change.

Why

tysers.com is organised entirely by product. A shipowner has to know to look separately under P&I, Hull & War, Cargo and Ports & Terminals, and nothing connects them. Clients tend to describe themselves by what they do rather than by the class of cover they buy.
