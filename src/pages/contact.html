Tell us a little about who you are and what you need, and your enquiry goes straight to the right team rather than a general inbox.

What we're proposing

One enquiry form that asks three routing questions before the message: who you are, what you need and which specialty it concerns. As the visitor answers, the form says which team it will go to. Arriving from a specialty page or a person's profile, it is already addressed.

A claim typed in here is pointed to the claims form, and press enquiries go to the press office.

Why

Today the same four fields (name, telephone, email, message) appear on Contact, Business Insurance, High-Net-Worth and in the footer of every page, and nothing asks what the enquiry is about. Someone at Tysers presumably reads each one and forwards it.

Try arriving from Cyber or from Richard Johnston's profile to see the prefill.
