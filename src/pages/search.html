Search

What we're proposing

Results grouped by what they are, with a count per group and a line of context on each. A claim-related search puts the claims route first. Try "claim", "Singapore", "cyber" or a person's name.

Why

On the live site a search for "claim" lists a news story about an appointment before the Claims page, then a run of product pages with no labels or summaries to tell them apart.

UmbracoBuilt on Examine with a field for content type, so results group and filter by type. Editors can pin a result to a term ("claim" pins Claims).
