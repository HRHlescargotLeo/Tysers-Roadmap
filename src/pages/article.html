The incident

At 8am, a member of the client's staff took a call from a UK number. The caller said they were from her organisation's IT team and that the company account had been compromised. To secure it, she was asked to install remote access software.

She hesitated, but the caller used the names of real colleagues and pressed the urgency, and she installed AnyDesk, a legitimate tool misused here. Once connected, the fraudster switched on privacy mode, blacking out her screen, and made several withdrawals totalling £80,000. Each was kept below £25,000 to avoid triggering a second authorisation.

The immediate response

She raised the alarm quickly. The company's IT provider removed the software, revoked access and secured the system. Tysers notified the insurer straight away, and the insurer offered a cyber fraud expert to support the recovery and validate the IT team's actions.
