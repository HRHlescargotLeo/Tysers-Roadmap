Notify a claim

Tell us what has happened. It takes about five minutes, you don't need your policy number, and your notification goes straight to the team that handles your class of cover.

What we're proposing

A four-step notification built in Umbraco Forms. The first step routes it; the rest collects only what the claims team needs to start. On sending, the visitor gets a reference and the name of the team, and the team's inbox gets the notification with attachments.

Coming from a specialty page or the claims finder, the first step is already filled in.

Why

There is currently no way to notify a claim online. A client finds a name in a list of 14 and phones, which is fine during office hours and if the number is right.

This doesn't replace the phone. It gives people a second route, and the claims team a consistent first record.
