Specialties A to Z

Every class of cover we place, in one list. Filter by division or by the industry you work in.

What we're proposing

One list of everything Tysers places, with a keyword box and two filters. In the rebuild each entry is one Specialty page in Umbraco; this list is generated from them.

The migration is also the moment to merge near-duplicates. The 79 entries here replace more than 100 product pages on the live site.

Why

The live site has three haulage and logistics pages, two political violence pages, nine prestige car pages and a Listed Building page at /isted-building-insurance/. There is no single list to check against, which is presumably how they accumulated.

BehaviourFilters apply as you type or choose. Links from menus open this page pre-filtered (try Industries, then Construction). An empty result offers a route to the enquiry form rather than a dead end.
