Search our brokers, claims specialists and leadership by name, specialty, team or office, and contact them directly.

What we're proposing

A people directory, built on the 135 team member records Tysers already keeps in WordPress. Each person has a profile with their role, specialties, office, contact details and anything they have written, and every specialty, claims and article page draws from the same records.

The prototype holds 28 of the people named publicly on tysers.com today, plus three placeholder entries where a team lead still needs naming.

Why

The live sitemap lists 135 profile URLs, such as /team_member/bob-pybus/, and every one we opened was an empty page set to be indexed by Google. So the data exists, and search engines are being told about it, but visitors can't use it.

For a broker, the person is usually the reason a client calls.

BehaviourResults update as you type. Contact details show only where the person has agreed to publish them; the rest show a placeholder. "Send an enquiry" opens the enquiry form addressed to that person.

Open questionWhich staff should be listed, and with which details? We'd suggest everyone client-facing, with a direct line and email, and a central number for anyone who prefers not to publish theirs.
