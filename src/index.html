Tysers reimagined

This is a model of tysers.com rebuilt on Umbraco. We already build and look after web apps for Tysers but not the website, so rather than suggest fixes to a site we don't run, we've modelled what it could look like if the rebuild and migration were used to make them all at once.

Content comes from the live site wherever it exists: the specialties, people, offices, claims contacts, reports and articles are Tysers' own. Where something new needs writing, it shows as [bracketed text]. This version applies the Tysers look (colours, condensed uppercase headings, square buttons and Tysers' own photography) on top of the structure agreed in the greyscale round. Univers is shown with a free stand-in font.

Five journeys to try

1

Find a specialist

Navigation by audience, specialty and industry that ends at a person you can contact, plus a people directory built on records Tysers already has.

Try: open the Industries menu, choose Shipping and maritime, then go from a specialist's card to their profile.

Open the directory
2

Notify a claim

Emergency numbers first, a claims-team finder by division and class, and an online notification that returns a reference.

Try: choose Tysers Live and Sport in the finder, then "Notify this team online" and leave the description empty.

Open Claims
3

The P&I Report

The flagship report given its own permanent page, with findings, previous editions and a sortable club comparison.

Try: sort the clubs by free reserves, then tick two to compare them.

Open the report
4

A specialty page

One template for all 79 specialties: facts, cover, the whole team with contact details, the class claims lead and related insight.

Try: after P&I, open Cyber from the Specialties menu, then pick anything from Specialties A to Z.

Open P&I
5

Make an enquiry

A form that asks who you are and what you need, says which team it will go to, and arrives prefilled from a specialty or a person.

Try: choose "An insurer, reinsurer or MGA", then change "What do you need?" to "To report a claim".

Open the form

How to review this

  • The dark bar at the top is the prototype's own navigation and isn't part of the proposed site. Its Notes switch shows, on every page, what we're proposing and why, plus yellow notes on behaviour and Umbraco, and red notes for questions we need Tysers to answer. Notes are off by default.

  • Menus, filters, forms, accordions, the claims finder, the club table and search all work. Nothing is sent anywhere. Links to individual policies, external sites and legal pages go nowhere at this stage.

  • Text in [brackets] is content that doesn't exist yet. Names, roles, offices and published phone numbers are as they appear on tysers.com today; numbers we think are wrong are marked "to confirm".

  • The P&I club figures are invented to show how the comparison works. The club names are real.

  • Photography is Tysers' own, loaded from tysers.com. Where a picture can't load, a navy panel stands in. Portraits, maps and charts are drawn on purpose.

  • Resize the browser or open it on a phone to see the mobile layout and menu.
